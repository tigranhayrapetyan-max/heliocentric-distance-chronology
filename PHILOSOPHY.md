# Project philosophy

> **Every scientific result should be reproducible, transparent, and honestly reported.**

## Purpose

Heliocentric Distance Chronology (HDC) provides a transparent computational basis for reconstructing, validating, and analysing heliocentric-distance chronologies from JPL Horizons ephemerides.

## Working principles

1. We do not claim more than the evidence supports.
2. Validation is part of the research, not an afterthought.
3. Generated outputs are not edited manually.
4. Scientific parameters are changed through version-controlled configuration files, and outputs are regenerated through the documented pipeline.
5. Uncertainty, negative results, and discrepancies are documented rather than hidden.
6. Software serves the scientific question; scientific hypotheses are not silently embedded in implementation details.
7. Trust is earned through reproducibility, transparency, and validation.

*Let come what will.*

## Frozen historical input
Use `ARMENIAN_HISTORICAL_MASTER_REGISTER_FROZEN_V1` without modification. No historical row, date, event class, polarity, source status, or cluster may be changed after astronomical matches are inspected. Rejected, undated, single-source-only, and context-only rows are excluded from temporal hypothesis tests.

## Confirmatory state-transition family
Primary state-transition sample = rows with `POOLING_STRATUM = STATE_TRANSITION` and `ANALYSIS_STATUS = PRIMARY`.

Fixed tests:
1. `STATE_ANY_PRIMARY × H1` — all primary state transitions.
2. `STATE_NEGATIVE_NARROW_PRIMARY × H1` — only `COLLAPSE + SOVEREIGNTY_LOSS`; this is the principal directional test.
3. `STATE_POSITIVE_PRIMARY × H1` — formation, independence, restoration, kingdom establishment, and modern independence-process events.

Sensitivity tests use PRIMARY + SENSITIVITY state transitions and clustered robustness with one deterministic representative per event cluster.

## Sovereign-accession family
Sovereign accessions are a separate secondary hypothesis family and are never pooled into the primary state-transition denominator. Primary and all-eligible accession analyses are reported separately, with clustered robustness.

## Other political-status strata
Regional/princely accessions, Zakarid/princely administrative strata, and melik events are sensitivity/descriptive only and are not merged with the primary state-transition test.

## Astronomical hierarchy
H1 is the primary exposure. H2/H3 are secondary exact-day root catalogues; their historical match rule is corrected separately in `H2_H3_DIRECT_HISTORICAL_DAY_RULE_CORRECTION_V2.md`. H4 exact and Pluto are secondary/descriptive constructs.

## Null model
Primary enrichment inference uses an exhaustive circular-shift null across the complete frozen astronomical year support. The historical configuration is shifted as a block, preserving relative spacing, labels, polarity, cluster membership, and interval widths. Enrichment p is the proportion of shifts with statistic greater than or equal to observed, including the observed shift.

## Interpretation rule
A null result means not supported in the frozen historical register under the frozen astronomical definition, not proof of absence. A positive secondary result cannot overturn a null principal negative-H1 test without independent replication. No historical repair or reclassification is permitted based on astronomical results.

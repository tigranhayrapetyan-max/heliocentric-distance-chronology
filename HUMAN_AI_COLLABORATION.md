# Human–AI collaborative development

This project was developed through a collaborative human–AI workflow. AI-assisted tools supported software implementation, documentation, quality assurance, debugging, and research-software engineering.

Scientific questions, hypotheses, methodological decisions, validation criteria, interpretation of results, and final approval of released materials remain the responsibility of the researcher and named human authors.

AI assistance is treated as an engineering and analytical tool. It does not replace scientific judgement, accountability, or authorship.
